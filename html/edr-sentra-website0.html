Response cycle

From first signal to closed case.

Every detection moves through the same four stages — automated where it's safe to be, escalated where it isn't.

01 — Detect

Continuous telemetry

Kernel-level sensors stream process, file, and network events with sub-second latency.

02 — Investigate

Automated correlation

Weak signals are chained into a single scored verdict, ranked by likely intent.

03 — Contain

Isolate the blast radius

Network isolation and process termination fire in milliseconds, no analyst required.

04 — Remediate

Guided recovery

Rollback scripts and a full root-cause report are handed to your team automatically.
